How lessons work

No rigid textbook path. Tell Maira what you want to play and she builds the plan.

  1. Book a trial

    A short intro call to talk goals, favorite songs, and where you are on guitar today.

  2. Get your plan

    A simple roadmap with songs, chords, and exercises matched to your level and taste.

  3. Play live together

    Weekly video lessons with demos, feedback on your playing, and clear tabs and chord charts.

  4. Practice and share

    Send a quick recording between lessons and get notes back so you keep improving.
